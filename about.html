About us

Little Tides Montessori is a family-centred Montessori preschool at 61 Minerva Road North, Ballito, for children from 18 months to 6 years. Our classrooms are calm, beautifully prepared spaces where children are trusted to explore, concentrate and do things for themselves. Like the tide, every child moves at their own rhythm, and with warm, skilful guidance they grow a little more each day.

Who we welcome

We welcome Ballito families of every background, and we treat each child as a unique person with their own rhythm of growing. Children from 18 months to 6 years.

Meet our teachers

The heart of any school is its teachers. Ours guide, encourage and notice the little things that matter.
