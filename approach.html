Our approach

Every child, in their own rhythm

A carefully prepared classroom, real work that little hands can do, and the freedom to concentrate.

What Montessori means for your child

It means a teacher who watches closely, steps in when it helps, and steps back when your child is ready to try alone. Independence, kindness and a love of learning are the work of every ordinary day. That is the heart of Little Tides Montessori, for children from 18 months to 6 years.

In a Montessori classroom, children are trusted to work at their own pace, with beautiful, purposeful materials and a teacher who observes, guides and encourages. These are practised every day, not as slogans but as habits built with care.

A day at Little Tides

A day at Little Tides is quietly full. Morning greetings. Meaningful work. Time outdoors. A shared meal. Rest when it is needed, and the small, proud moment when a child does something for themselves for the first time. We would love to show you around.

School hours: All sessions start at 07:30. Half day until 12:15 (without lunch) or 13:30 (with lunch); full day until 17:30.
