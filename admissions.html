Admissions

Enrolments are open

If you are looking for a warm Montessori preschool in Ballito, we would love to meet you.

How to enrol

Start with a short enquiry or an application on our Enquire page, come and see us, and we will be in touch if a place may be available. Please do not send payment with an enquiry or application.

All sessions start at 07:30.

  • Fees are paid for 12 months and are due by the 1st of the month.
  • Half day excluding lunch ends at 12:15 and does not include lunch.
  • Half day includes a cooked lunch and ends at 13:30.
  • Full day includes a cooked lunch as well as a mid-afternoon snack and ends at 17:30.
  • Aftercare runs from 13:30 until 17:30 and includes an afternoon snack. The annual fee is R8 400, or R700 a month (over 12 months). Ad hoc aftercare is R110 a day after 13:30, billed at the end of the month.
  • The holiday programme runs throughout the school holidays. The annual fee is R4 800, or R400 a month (over 12 months). A change to include or exclude the holiday programme can only be made at the beginning of a term. Ad hoc holiday care is R250 a half day or R320 a full day, billed at the end of the holiday, if a package that excludes the holiday programme is selected.
  • Pay the full year upfront and receive an 8% discount. There is no deadline: the discount applies whenever the full year is paid upfront.
  • A 7% discount applies for a second or third sibling who attends at the same time.
  • One month’s fees (refundable deposit) are due to secure your child’s place.
  • A term’s written notice (four months), or a full term’s fees in lieu of notice, is required if you wish to withdraw your child from the school.
  • Extra-murals are charged separately and must be organised directly with the respective providers. Optional educational talks, medical check-ups (hearing or sight tests) and class photos are also charged separately.
  • Any late payment (later than the 15th of the month) incurs a late-payment administration fee of R300.00 a month. If fees are not paid by the 15th of the month, attendance may be suspended until the account is up to date and the late-payment fee has been paid. The suspension period is still payable, and notice is still required if the child is withdrawn.
