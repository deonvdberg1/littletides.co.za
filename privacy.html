Privacy

Protection of your family’s information

This page explains how we look after the personal information of children, parents and guardians.

Little Tides Montessori Ballito looks after the personal information of children, parents and guardians with care. This notice explains how we do that. If anything is unclear, you are welcome to ask us.

Who is responsible

The responsible party is Little Tides (Pty) Ltd t/a Little Tides Montessori, of 61 Minerva Road North, Ballito, KwaZulu-Natal (“Little Tides”, “we”, “us”).

Our Information Officer is Deon van den Berg, Director, info@littletides.co.za.

This school is for children from 18 months to 6 years. Because we work with young children, we only process a child’s information with the consent of a parent or legal guardian (a “competent person”), or where the law otherwise allows.

Why we collect information

We collect and use personal information to:

  • consider and process your application or enrolment, and enter into a contract with you for a place at the school;
  • care for, teach and support your child day to day, and keep in touch with you about school life;
  • look after your child’s health, safety and wellbeing, including medication, allergies, emergencies and incident records;
  • meet our legal duties, including those under the Children’s Act 38 of 2005 and related early childhood development and partial-care requirements, labour and tax law (for any employment matters), and any duties to the Department of Social Development, the provincial education department, or the local municipality;
  • keep the records the law and good practice require, and to deal with questions, complaints or claims; and
  • send you school communications. Separate, optional marketing (for example, a newsletter to families who are not yet enrolled) will only happen if you agree to it.

What information we may hold

  • About the parent or guardian: name, identity or passport number, contact details, address, relationship to the child, emergency contacts, and fee-payer or billing details.
  • About the child: name, date of birth, identity number (if they have one), language, admission details, attendance, and developmental or progress notes.
  • Special personal information and children’s information, processed with your consent and only where needed to care for your child or to meet a legal duty. This can include health and medical information (allergies, conditions, medication, immunisation information, doctor or medical-aid details, and records of illness or injury at school), and any information you give us about additional support needs.

Please give us information that is accurate and up to date, and tell us promptly if it changes.

Who we may share it with

We do not sell personal information. We only share it where it is needed for the purposes above, and then only as much as is necessary. This may include staff and service providers who help us run the school, under agreements that require them to keep the information safe; medical professionals or emergency services if your child needs urgent care; authorities where the law requires or allows us to share information; and our professional advisers where needed.

Information stored outside South Africa

Some of the services we use to run the school, including our email and the online enquiry and application forms, may store information on servers outside South Africa. We will take the steps the law requires when that happens, and we limit what is stored to what we need.

How long we keep it

We keep information only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or for the periods the law requires. We then delete or safely destroy the records, or keep only what we must.

How we look after it

We take reasonable steps to keep personal information secure. This includes limiting access to staff who need it for their work, using password-protected systems and locked files where appropriate, and asking our service providers to do the same. No system is perfectly secure, but we will tell you and the Information Regulator if the law requires us to report a security compromise.

Your rights

You may ask whether we hold personal information and request a copy; ask us to correct or update it; object to certain processing, or ask us to delete information, where the law allows; withdraw consent at any time where we rely on consent; and lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To use these rights, please contact our Information Officer, Deon van den Berg, at info@littletides.co.za, or write to us at 61 Minerva Road North, Ballito.

Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za · enquiries@inforegulator.org.za · 010 023 5200 or 0800 017 160.
