Parent FAQ

Questions families often ask

Here are answers to the questions parents ask most.

What ages do you take?

Children from 18 months to 6 years.

Where are you?

61 Minerva Road North, Ballito.

What are your school hours?

All sessions start at 07:30. Half day until 12:15 (without lunch) or 13:30 (with lunch); full day until 17:30.

When can my child start?

Enrolments are open until all places are filled. The 2027 school year starts on Wednesday, 13 January 2027, in line with the KwaZulu-Natal public school calendar.

What is Montessori?

A calm, prepared classroom where children learn by doing, at their own pace, guided by a teacher who observes closely.

How do I enrol?

You can apply now, or contact us to book a visit. If you have a question, you are welcome to call, WhatsApp or email info@littletides.co.za.

Can I visit first?

Yes, please do. Contact us at info@littletides.co.za to book a visit.
