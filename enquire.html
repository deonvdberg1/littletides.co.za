Privacy notice

Thank you for thinking of Little Tides Montessori Ballito. If you send us this form, Little Tides (Pty) Ltd (reg. 2026/922192/07) t/a Little Tides Montessori, of 61 Minerva Road North, Ballito, will use the details you give us only to reply to your enquiry, share information you have asked for about our school (for children aged 18 months to 6 years), and, if you ask us to, follow up about a visit or a place.

We usually need your name, contact details, and any notes you choose to add about your child (for example, their first name and age). Please do not include health or other sensitive details on this form unless you feel you must. If you do include them, we will treat them carefully and only use them to respond to your enquiry.

We will not sell your details. We will not add you to a marketing list unless you separately agree. We keep enquiry records only for as long as we need them to deal with your question and to follow our usual records practice, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. You may ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and you may withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to our Information Officer, Deon van den Berg, at info@littletides.co.za. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Photo and media consent is not asked on this enquiry form. It belongs in the enrolment pack. Please also see our Privacy notice.
