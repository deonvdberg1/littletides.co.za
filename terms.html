Fee Terms and Conditions

These terms apply to school fees, deposits and related charges. This page is a draft and is not yet published.

Little Tides (Pty) Ltd, reg. 2026/922192/07, t/a Little Tides Montessori, of 61 Minerva Road North, Ballito. 072 456 1282 · info@littletides.co.za.

Programme fees are set out in the fee schedule on our Admissions page.

1. School fees

  • Annual school fees are payable in 12 monthly instalments, in advance by the 1st of each month.
  • All sessions start at 07:30.
  • Programmes:
    • Half day until 12:15, without lunch.
    • Half day until 13:30, including a cooked lunch.
    • Full day until 17:30, including a cooked lunch and an afternoon snack.
  • Monthly fees for each programme, including the 3-day and 4-day options, are set out in the fee schedule on the Admissions page.
  • Payment is by EFT only, using your child’s account number as the reference.

2. Deposit

  • A refundable deposit equal to one month’s fees is payable to secure your child’s place. The deposit is refunded once the account is settled in full and notice has been served.

3. Discounts

  • A 7% discount applies to the second or third sibling attending the school at the same time. The sibling discount and the 8% upfront discount can be combined.
  • Pay the full year upfront and receive an 8% discount. The discount applies whenever the full year is paid upfront. The upfront discount also applies to the annual aftercare and holiday programme fees.

4. Aftercare

  • Aftercare runs from 13:30 to 17:30 and includes an afternoon snack. It costs R700 a month, payable in advance by the 1st of the month.
  • Ad hoc aftercare costs R110 a day and is billed at the end of the month.

5. Holiday programme

  • The holiday programme runs throughout the school holidays. The annual fee is payable in 12 monthly payments of R400 by the 1st of the month.
  • A change to include or exclude the holiday programme can only be made at the beginning of a term.
  • Children on a package that excludes the holiday programme may attend ad hoc at R250 a half day or R320 a full day, billed at the end of the holiday.

6. Late payment

  • Fees not received by the 15th of the month incur a late payment administration fee of R300 a month.
  • If fees are not paid by the 15th of the month, attendance is suspended until the account is up to date and the late payment fee has been paid.
  • Fees remain payable as normal during any suspension. Suspension is not an expulsion.
  • The school may refuse admittance to any child whose fees are not paid by the due date.

7. Withdrawal

  • One term’s written notice (four months), or a full term’s fees in lieu of notice, is required to withdraw a child from the school.
  • Notice is still required if a child is withdrawn during a period of suspension.

8. Extras

  • Extra-murals are arranged and paid for directly with the providers. The school does not invoice any extra-murals.
  • Optional educational talks, medical screenings (for example hearing and sight tests) and class photos are charged separately.
